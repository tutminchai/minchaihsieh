02 ABOUT

AboutResearch meets teaching.

Dr. Min-Chai Hsieh earned his Ph.D. from the Department of Digital Learning Technology at the National University of Tainan and currently serves as an Associate Professor in the Department of Multimedia and Animation at the Tainan University of Technology. He was honored as an "Outstanding Teacher in the Teacher Evaluation for the 2025 Academic Year" and recognized under the "2026 Program for Recruiting and Retaining Exceptional and Outstanding Talent." He serves as an advisory professor for the Ministry of Education's 2026 AI Talent Ark program, its 2026 initiative integrating immersive technology into competency-oriented teaching, and its 2026 academic-year program promoting technology-assisted self-directed learning and digital teaching in senior high schools. His previous educational service includes advising schools under the MOE's digital learning advancement program and serving as an advisory professor and project reviewer for its 5G new technology demonstration school program. He was awarded the Distinguished Alumnus of National Pingtung University in 2016 and the College-Level Teaching Excellence Award from the Tainan University of Technology for the 2020 Academic Year.

His current research interests include artificial intelligence applications, digital learning, Extended Reality (Mixed Reality, Augmented Reality, and Virtual Reality), the educational metaverse, and interaction design. He is dedicated to integrating XR technology with digital media design and applying XR to digital media content creation. To date, he has been invited to deliver 183 lectures and workshops. He has published over 70 conference and journal papers, including those indexed in SSCI, SCIE, and EI, and regularly serves as a reviewer for international journals.

He has authored six books: Virtual Reality in the Metaverse: Building Your VR World from Scratch (published in April 2025), Shuttling Through AR in the Metaverse: Emerging Technologies Combining Reality and Virtuality (published on March 25, 2023), AR Unity Augmented Reality Practical Development (published on October 4, 2021), Easy Learning of AR Augmented Reality: New Technology Applications Combining Virtuality and Reality (published on July 23, 2021), Fun with AR Augmented Reality! New Technology Applications Combining Virtuality and Reality (published on August 23, 2016), and Unity 3D Game Design from Scratch (published on August 27, 2015). He possesses extensive experience in integrating artificial intelligence with digital media interactive design, digital textbook design, and the practical application of virtual and augmented reality.
